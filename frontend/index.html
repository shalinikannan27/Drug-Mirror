Real-World Data (FAERS)

Inspect safety event signals using Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) scores calculated from the FDA adverse event database, and capped based on cross-validated AUC benchmarks.

Rigorous & Transparent Scoring

We run Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, 2048-bit) and XGBoost models under a multi-level confidence scoring architecture (High, Medium, and Low).

Confidence tags represent both model reliability (measured cross-validation metrics) and compound validity. Inputs outside our core validated drug datasets are automatically flagged as low-confidence.

High

Validated Compound

Predictions made on fully documented therapeutics with deep training overlap.

Medium

High Structural Overlap

Predictions on compounds structurally analogous to SIDER and Davis profiles.

Low

Low Reliability / Capped Categories

Target models displaying low predictive power (e.g. AUC below 0.60), or non-standard compound structures.
